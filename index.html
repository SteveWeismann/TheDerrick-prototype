Venango County commissioners approve $2.4M bridge repair contract
The county's oldest steel truss bridge will undergo structural reinforcement this fall, with completion expected before the first freeze.
LM
Luka Muller
June 10, 2026 · 4 min read
3 free articles remaining this month Subscribe →

Venango County commissioners voted 2-1 Tuesday to award a $2.4 million contract to Allegheny Bridge Works for emergency structural repairs on the Kennerdell Road span, the county's last remaining steel truss bridge and a critical connector for roughly 4,200 daily commuters.

The vote came after months of engineering assessments that found significant corrosion in the bridge's lower chord members — the horizontal beams that bear the primary load — and recommended repairs be completed before winter conditions worsen the deterioration.

This story took 6 hours to report. Support independent Venango County journalism.

Commissioner Sarah Deluca, who cast the dissenting vote, argued the county should have sought additional bids. "We had one qualified respondent," she said. "That's not a competitive process." County engineer Tom Braddock countered that the specialized nature of truss bridge repair limited the contractor pool statewide.

Commissioner Sarah Deluca, who cast the dissenting vote, argued the county should have sought additional bids. "We had one qualified respondent," she said. "That's not a competitive process." County engineer Tom Braddock countered that the specialized nature of truss bridge repair limited the contractor pool statewide.

Construction is scheduled to begin September 8 and run through November 15, with a two-week contingency window built into the contract. The bridge will remain open to traffic during off-peak hours, with single-lane alternating closures between 9 AM and 3 PM on weekdays.

The project is funded through a combination of state liquid fuels funds and a federal bridge program grant secured by the county's grants coordinator in March. Local taxpayers will cover approximately $340,000 of the total cost.
